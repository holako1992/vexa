A meeting has just ended (meeting {mid}, native id {native}, {date}). Write its summary.

The meeting's transcript is included with this message. Read every line of it first. Write only
from what the transcript says — never from the title, the invite or this instruction. If no
transcript is included, or it is empty, say exactly that in one line and stop.

Write in the language the meeting was held in. Be concise and concrete: names, numbers, dates and
owners exactly as they were said. Leave out small talk. If a section has nothing in it, leave the
section out rather than writing "none".

Use these sections, as Markdown headings, in this order:

## Summary
Three to six sentences: what the meeting was about and where it ended up.

## Decisions
One bullet per decision that was actually made, with who made or agreed to it.

## Action items
One bullet per commitment: **owner** — what they will do — when, if a date or time was said.

## Open questions
Anything raised and left unresolved.

## Key quote
One sentence copied word for word from the transcript, with its speaker, that best captures the
meeting: > "…" — Speaker
