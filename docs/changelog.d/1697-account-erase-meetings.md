- **Account deletion: the meetings service can now erase a whole account (#1697).** `POST /internal/accounts/{user_id}/erase` (internal secret, service-to-service only) stops the user's running and planned bots, deletes their meetings, transcripts and sessions, removes every recording from storage and the database, clears the meeting streams and sync keys in Redis, and takes the user out of transcripts other people shared with them. It is safe to retry after a failure and reports what each call removed.
