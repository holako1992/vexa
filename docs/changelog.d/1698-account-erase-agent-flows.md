- **Account erasure in the agent and flows domains (#1698).** agent-api and flows-api each gain an internal, idempotent `POST /internal/accounts/{subject}/erase` the identity service calls when an account is deleted. The agent domain removes the person's workspaces, private system area, stored repository token, chat sessions, warm units and scheduled routines (shared workspaces stay with their remaining members, with ownership handed on); the flows domain cancels their queued reactions (so no "meeting ready" or dunning email can still send) and deletes their reactions, receipts, mail threads and sender records. Both are internal-tier only and unreachable through the gateway.
